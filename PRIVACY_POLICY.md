# Apocalyptic AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 18, 2026**

This policy describes the Apocalyptic AI Android app. The app is a local-model preparedness assistant. It does not create user accounts, operate a cloud AI inference service, advertising network, or analytics service.

## Data stored on the device

Chat messages, saved conversations, settings, checklist state, downloaded model files, downloaded map files, and Survival Manual preferences are stored in app-specific storage on the Android device. Android backup is disabled. Users can clear app data, remove models and maps, or uninstall the app.

Emergency contact names and numbers are stored locally only when entered in the No-Signal SOS feature. The app does not read the phone address book. GPS is accessed only after the user activates a location control and grants permission. Coordinates remain on-device and are included in an SOS message only if the user chooses to copy or open an SMS composer.

## Network and third-party services

The app uses the network only for user-requested model and offline-map downloads and for links opened in an external browser or app. Selected file hosts may receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, requested resource, and request time. Their policies apply. Prompts are not sent to a remote AI inference server by Apocalyptic AI.

Optional spoken replies use the Android system text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio or provide voice dictation. External services opened by the user have their own data practices.

## Permissions

Internet permission supports downloads and external links. Location permission is requested only for GPS controls. The app does not request address-book, SMS-send, camera, microphone, or phone-state permission. Android notification permission may be requested for download notifications on supported versions. The user chooses whether an SMS message is sent; the app cannot confirm delivery.

## Retention and deletion

There is no server-side account or chat database. Local data remains until the user removes it, clears app data, or uninstalls the app, subject to Android behavior. For privacy questions, use the public release repository's [issue form](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/issues/new). Do not post private or sensitive information in a public issue.

## AI and safety

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or unsafe. Apocalyptic AI is not an emergency service and is not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice. Follow local law, official emergency instructions, and qualified professional guidance.

## Changes

This policy must be updated when the app's data practices change. The in-app policy, this public URL, release notes, and Play Console Data safety declarations should be kept consistent.
