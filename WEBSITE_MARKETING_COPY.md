## What Apocalyptic AI does

Apocalyptic AI combines local AI assistance with practical offline preparedness tools. It is designed for people who want useful information available on their phone before travel, during outages, in remote areas, or when internet access is unreliable.

### Private local AI chat

Download an optional model and run conversations on the device. Chat history is stored locally in app-specific storage. Prompts are not sent to a remote Apocalyptic AI inference server.

### Offline field guide

Search practical reminders for water, shelter, first aid, food safety, navigation, communications, evacuation, and essential supplies. The field guide is reference material and does not replace official instructions or trained professionals.

### Survival Manual

Browse the embedded offline Survival Manual experience with chapters, search, illustrations, reading preferences, and offline access. The manual retains its upstream notices and disclaimers.

### Offline maps

Download selected map regions for use when connectivity is limited. Saved map files remain in the app's private storage and can be removed from the Offline Maps screen.

### No-Signal SOS Kit

Prepare an emergency message, save emergency contacts locally, request a GPS fix when you choose, and hand a message off to the phone's SMS app. Apocalyptic AI does not claim that a message was delivered; you decide whether to send it.

### Go-bag checklist

Keep a practical preparedness checklist on your phone and review essential supplies before travel or an emergency.

### Flexible model choices

Choose from supported on-device models based on download size and device capability. Models are optional downloads and are governed by their own publishers' licenses and terms.

## Built for privacy

Apocalyptic AI does not require an account. Chat history, preferences, emergency contacts, downloaded models, and downloaded maps are stored locally on the device. Android backup is disabled for app data. Location is requested only when you activate a GPS control. The app does not read your address book and does not request SMS-send permission.

Optional spoken replies use the Android text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Apocalyptic AI does not operate an advertising network, analytics service, or cloud AI chat backend.

Read the complete [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## Important safety notice

Apocalyptic AI is a general preparedness tool, not an emergency service, doctor, lawyer, financial adviser, or replacement for official local guidance. AI-generated answers can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, biased, or unsafe. For urgent situations, contact local emergency services and follow current government, public-health, weather, utility, and professional instructions.

Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or other people's confidential information into the app. Verify important information before acting on it.

Read the complete [AI Safety Notice](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/blob/main/AI_SAFETY_NOTICE.md).

## FAQ

### Does Apocalyptic AI need internet access?

The core field guide, Survival Manual, checklist, saved conversations, and already-downloaded model data are designed for local use. Internet access is used for user-requested model and map downloads and for links that you choose to open.

### Are my chats uploaded?

No. The selected model runs on the device, and Apocalyptic AI does not send prompts to a cloud inference server. Local Android storage and the selected model provider's download service have separate privacy considerations.

### Does the app send an SOS automatically?

No. The app prepares information and can hand an SMS message to the phone's messaging app. You choose whether to send it, and the app cannot confirm delivery.

### Is the APK from the official project?

The official public distribution repository is [Apocalyptic-AI-Releases](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases). Download releases from that repository and verify the version before installing.

### Is the app a replacement for emergency services?

No. Apocalyptic AI is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services for urgent danger and follow official instructions.

### Where can I report a problem?

Use the public [complaint and issue form](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/issues/new). Do not post passwords, private user data, GPS coordinates, confidential legal documents, or security-sensitive details in a public issue.

### Can rights holders request a takedown?

Yes. Follow the [Takedown and Support process](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/blob/main/TAKEDOWN_AND_SUPPORT.md) and provide only the information needed for review.
