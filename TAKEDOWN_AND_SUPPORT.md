# Takedown, Copyright, Privacy, and Support Requests

This repository is a public distribution point for Apocalyptic AI APK release files and public notices. If you believe an APK, document, logo, or other material infringes your rights or violates an applicable license, report it promptly so the maintainer can review it.

## What to include

Open a GitHub issue with the affected release URL or file path, a clear description of the work or right involved, your contact information, and a statement that you have a good-faith belief the material is unauthorized or inaccurate. For copyright notices, identify the original work, the allegedly infringing material, and the basis for your authority to act. Include only information necessary for review; do not post personal IDs, passwords, private user data, or confidential legal documents publicly.

For privacy or security-sensitive matters, do not disclose details in a public issue. Start with a minimal issue asking for a private contact route, or follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

## Review process

The maintainer may temporarily hide or replace a release while reviewing a credible request. A request does not automatically establish infringement or require removal. The maintainer may ask for clarification, preserve relevant records, or contact affected contributors and licensors. Counter-notices and license evidence may be considered where appropriate.

This repository is not a law firm and this page is general process information, not legal advice. Nothing here waives rights or remedies under applicable law.

## General support

For installation, accessibility, release, or policy questions, use the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/sheikhdipuraihan-sudo/Apocalyptic-AI-Releases/issues). Do not expect GitHub issues to provide emergency support; contact local emergency services for emergencies.
