# AI Safety Notice

Apocalyptic AI provides general preparedness information generated partly by an on-device language model. It can hallucinate, misunderstand context, omit critical details, or provide unsafe advice.

Do not use it as an emergency dispatcher, medical professional, legal adviser, financial adviser, navigation guarantee, or replacement for official instructions. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services. Verify advice against current government, public-health, utility, weather, and professional sources.

Users should not use the app to plan violence, evade law enforcement, create weapons, harm themselves or others, or make high-stakes decisions without qualified human oversight. Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or another person's confidential information into the app.

Model downloads are optional and are governed by the selected model publisher's license and terms. A model being listed in the app does not mean Apocalyptic AI owns or endorses all generated output or third-party model content.

If an output appears dangerous, discriminatory, illegal, or otherwise inappropriate, do not rely on it. Report the release and describe the issue without posting private data, secrets, or harmful instructions in a public issue.
